# Product Requirements Document

## Product
Northstar Pricing Studio

## Fictional company
Northstar Analytics

## Problem statement
Northstar's commercial team currently relies on spreadsheets, manually maintained pricing guidance, and proposal templates. Standard deals require repeated data entry and manual calculations, while nonstandard deals create approval ambiguity.

## Primary users

### Account Executive
Configure a standard deal and generate a professional proposal without needing a pricing expert for every request.

### Pricing / Commercial Strategy
Maintain pricing logic in a governed place, understand exceptions, and evolve pricing without rewriting the entire application.

### Finance
Understand how final pricing was derived and verify that discounts and adjustments comply with policy.

## MVP user journey
1. Enter customer information.
2. Select subscription tier.
3. Configure users, coverage, history, term, services, and renewal status.
4. Enter requested discount.
5. Calculate quote.
6. Review itemized adjustments.
7. Resolve validation issues or approval flags.
8. Save quote.
9. Generate proposal-ready output.

## Product principles
1. Configuration over hard-coding.
2. Explainability over cleverness.
3. Governed self-service over unrestricted flexibility.
4. Useful MVP over exhaustive functionality.
5. Every output should be reproducible.
